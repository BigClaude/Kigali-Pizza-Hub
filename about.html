Umuco w’i Napoli Wakiriwe i Kigali

Ubwiza n’Ubuhanga bwo Guteka Pizza

Umuco wo guteka pizza ya Neapolitan wemejwe na UNESCO nk’umurage ndangamuco w’ikiremwamuntu. Hano kuri ITALIANO PIZZA Kigali, twazanye uyu mwimerere wose i Rwanda, twubaka ifuru y’amabuye ya kimeza i Nyarutarama.

Ntitukoresha ubusemburo bw’inganda cyangwa gaze; dukoresha gusa amaboko y’abahanga, ifu y’ingano y’umwimerere imara amasaha 72 itegurwa, n’inkwi z’amabuye zaka umuriro usukuye.

Inzira y’Ubwiza

Uburyo Ifu Yacu Itegugwa mu Masaha 72

Impamvu pizza yacu itaremereza igifu kandi ikaryohera umuryango wose.

01. KUVANGA (ISAHA 0)

Ifu n’Amazi Yonyine

Ifu y’ingano nshya ivangwa n’amazi meza y’isoko, igategerezwa kugira ngo ifarini ibanze inywe amazi neza mbere yo gushyiramo umunyu.

02. UBUSEMBURO (AMASAHA 1–24)

Ubusemburo bw’Umwimerere

Dukoresha umusemburo wa kimeza (wild sourdough starter) ukora uburyohe bwa kamere n’impumuro nziza itagereranywa.

03. GUHURA (AMASAHA 24–72)

Gukonjesha ku gipimo cya 4°C

Ifu ibumbwe neza mu maduce ikomeza kuruhuka ahantu hakonje, ibi bituma isukari y’ingano icika intege n’ifu ikoroha cyane mu nda.

04. GUTSEKA (MASEGONDA 65)

Ubushyuhe bwa 485°C

Pizza itekwa mu masegonda 65 gusa ku mabuye y’ifuru ashyushye, igasohoka irabagirana kandi yoroshye nk’umutsima woroheje.
